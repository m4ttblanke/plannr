The setup you keep redoing

Every term brings four to six syllabi, each in its own format, each with a dozen dates scattered through it — readings, labs, quizzes, a midterm, a final.

Copying all of that into a calendar by hand is slow, easy to get wrong, and you do it again the moment a professor moves a deadline. Miss one line and you miss the assignment.

The part Plannr does for you

From a syllabus you scroll past
to a calendar you actually use

Read → extract → review → organized. You stay in control at the step that matters.

Every course in one list, each with its own color and sync status. Group them into terms.

See a heavy week coming before it lands. Counts per day, plus a weekend preview.

When things change

You don’t start over

Grouped by term

File classes into a quarter, semester, or custom-length term. When the term ends, its classes go inactive on their own and drop out of your week view.

Re-upload when the syllabus changes

Plannr compares the new version against what’s already there and syncs only the difference. Edits you made by hand in Google Calendar stay put, and every sync is saved so you can roll a class back.

Read the full Privacy Policy
  • Review every date before it syncs. Accept, edit, or decline each extracted event individually.
  • Re-uploads keep your manual edits. Reconciling a changed syllabus preserves changes you made directly in Google Calendar.
  • Every sync is restorable. Plannr snapshots each sync so you can roll a class back to an earlier version.
  • Your syllabus text isn’t kept. It’s used to find dates and isn’t stored after processing; Google credentials are encrypted and used only server-side.
