2. Description of the Service

Plannr is a student productivity tool that helps you organize your course schedule. The app allows you to:

  • Upload course syllabus documents (PDF format)
  • Automatically extract key dates such as assignments, exams, quizzes, and lab deadlines
  • Review and manage the extracted events
  • Sync events directly to your Google Calendar

Plannr uses Google OAuth to authenticate your account and the Google Calendar API to create events on your behalf.
